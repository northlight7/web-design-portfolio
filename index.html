A study in
digital storytelling.

Each project explores a different relationship between space, movement and interaction: a landscape you climb, an object you turn over, a programme you assemble, a record you set down, a bloom you open, a house you light.

All six are independent concept demos, created with AI-assisted design and development, iterative art direction and browser testing. No client work, no commercial results and no real products are claimed. The notes include the design idea, the interactions, desktop and mobile screenshots and the source code. Every live site opens directly in your browser, with no account and no paid service.
